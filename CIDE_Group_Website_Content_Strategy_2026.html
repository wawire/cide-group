Project Overview

CIDE Group (registered as COMMUNE IDEE (CIDE) GROUPE LTD) is a pan-African advisory, consulting, and implementation organisation with over a decade of experience across development, humanitarian, and business sectors. Despite this depth of expertise, the organisation lacks a digital presence that reflects it. This project delivers a fully designed and built static website at www.cidegroup.org that positions CIDE as a credible, modern, and compelling advisory partner across Africa and globally.

Why This Matters Now
The opportunity is urgent: In the development advisory sector, a website is a procurement tool as much as a marketing tool. When tender evaluators, donor programme managers, and potential consortium partners look CIDE up before shortlisting — and they do — the current digital absence is a credibility gap that is actively costing opportunities. This project closes that gap.

Why a Static Site?

Advantages for CIDE

  • Zero hosting cost on platforms like GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel
  • Extremely fast — no server-side processing or database queries
  • Highly secure — no CMS login credentials to compromise
  • Version-controlled via Git — every change tracked, every version recoverable
  • Easy to hand to any developer for future updates
  • No licence fees, plugin costs, or CMS subscription overheads

Constraints to Plan For

  • Content updates require a developer or someone comfortable editing code/Markdown
  • Form handling requires a third-party service (e.g. Formspree, Netlify Forms)
  • No built-in blog/CMS — a headless CMS can be added post-launch if needed
  • Dynamic filtering (e.g. project portfolio) requires client-side JavaScript
  • Training the communications team on how to request and review updates is essential

Brand Positioning & Messaging

CIDE Group occupies a specific and defensible position in the pan-African advisory market: locally rooted, evidence-based, and multi-disciplinary. The website must not merely describe services — it must communicate why CIDE is the right choice over both international advisory firms (which lack local context) and smaller local consultancies (which lack breadth and systems). Every page must actively reinforce this positioning.

Core Positioning Statement
Positioning — for internal use only, not direct website copy CIDE Group is the pan-African advisory partner that combines decade-long continent-wide expertise with genuine local rootedness — delivering practical, evidence-driven solutions that international firms design from distance and local firms cannot scale. We work at the intersection of innovation, policy, enterprise, and community, guided by a 360° model from research through to evaluation.
Positioning Framework

Brand Promise

"Thriving Together" — CIDE Group does not deliver to communities; it works with them. Every service, every project, and every intervention is anchored in local knowledge, collaborative design, and a genuine commitment to leaving systems stronger than we found them.

Unique Value Proposition

The only pan-African advisory organisation that offers fully integrated services across strategic communications, ICT, MEL, research, programme design, capacity building, enterprise support, and policy advocacy — all under one roof, guided by a signature 360° methodology.

Tone of Voice

Confident without arrogance. African-rooted without being parochial. Evidence-led without being dry. Every sentence should feel written by a practitioner who has been in the field — not by a marketing team selling from a boardroom. Clear, direct, and solution-focused.

What We Do Not Claim

Avoid superlatives without evidence ("the leading," "the best"). Do not overstate geographic reach without confirmed project presence. Do not use development jargon without explanation for non-specialist audiences. Avoid passive voice — CIDE acts, CIDE delivers, CIDE builds.

Content Pillars

Every page, paragraph, and CTA on the CIDE Group website must serve one of four purposes. These pillars are not just editorial categories — they are questions to ask about every piece of content before it is written, approved, or published. If content does not clearly serve a pillar, it should be cut or rewritten.

Credibility & Trust

  • Named clients, partners, and project references
  • Measurable results and impact statistics
  • Team profiles with credentials and sector experience
  • Case studies showing problem → approach → outcome
  • Over-a-decade heritage reference
  • 360° model as a proof of rigour
  • Knowledge Hub publications and research outputs

Services & Capability Clarity

  • Plain-language service descriptions (no jargon)
  • Specific offerings per service (not just categories)
  • Who we serve — audience-specific language per page
  • 360° approach integrated into service descriptions
  • Focus area × service combinations (e.g. GEDSI + MEL)
  • Downloadable capability statement
  • FAQs where complex services need explanation

Action & Conversion

  • Contact / Work With Us CTA on every service page
  • Partnership and proposal enquiry form
  • Downloadable capability statement (lead magnet)
  • Email address prominent in footer and contact
  • WhatsApp/phone link on mobile for instant contact
  • LinkedIn follow CTA on all pages
  • "Submit a tender enquiry" prompt in header

Thought Leadership & Visibility

  • Monthly blog articles on development themes
  • Policy briefs and research publications
  • Toolkits and practical guides for partners
  • Expert opinions on sector developments
  • Event recaps and conference participation
  • Africa-specific insights that competitors miss
  • SEO-optimised content driving organic discovery

Static Site Architecture & Tech Stack

The website will be built as a static site — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no server-side database or CMS dependency at launch. This is the right approach for CIDE at this stage: zero ongoing infrastructure cost, high performance, easy version control, and a simple path to adding a lightweight headless CMS in Phase 2 if non-technical content editing becomes a priority. The recommended stack below is production-proven for development sector websites of this scale.

Recommended Technology Stack
Core Build Stack
Static Site GeneratorHugo (recommended) or Eleventy (11ty) — both are fast, free, and well-supported. Hugo compiles the full site in under 1 second. Templates are written in HTML with simple data injection via Markdown files. No Node.js runtime in production.
StylingPlain CSS or SCSS — no CSS framework dependency. Tailwind CSS is acceptable if the developer prefers it. Avoid Bootstrap — too heavy and too generic for a distinctive brand site.
JavaScriptVanilla JS only — no React, Vue, or Angular. Portfolio filter, accordion FAQ, mobile nav, and scroll animations can all be achieved in under 50KB of vanilla JS.
HostingNetlify (recommended) — free tier supports CIDE's traffic volume, includes SSL, CDN, form handling (Netlify Forms), and Git-based continuous deployment. Vercel is an equivalent alternative.
Version ControlGitHub — private repository. All code changes committed via pull request. Every version of the site is recoverable. The communications team can review and approve changes before they go live.
Domain / DNSwww.cidegroup.org — confirm registrar and DNS access. Point CNAME to Netlify. SSL certificate auto-provisioned via Let's Encrypt on Netlify. Zero cost.
Contact FormsNetlify Forms (if hosting on Netlify — free for up to 100 submissions/month) or Formspree (free tier: 50 submissions/month, paid: $10/month). All form submissions delivered to communications.cidegroup@gmail.com or a dedicated @cidegroup.org address.
AnalyticsGoogle Analytics 4 — implemented via gtag.js with a cookie consent gate. Google Search Console for keyword and indexation monitoring. Both free.
Image OptimisationHugo's built-in image processing pipeline, or manual optimisation via Squoosh.app. All images served as WebP with JPEG fallback. Lazy loading on all below-fold images.
Professional EmailMigrate from communications.cidegroup@gmail.com to a @cidegroup.org address. Zoho Mail (free for up to 5 users) or Google Workspace ($6/user/month). Critical for credibility with development partners and tender evaluators.

If non-technical content editing is needed post-launch: Decap CMS (formerly Netlify CMS) or CloudCannon can be added to the Hugo site without any redevelopment. Editors get a simple browser-based interface for editing Markdown content files — no code required. The static site architecture is preserved, and there is no additional hosting cost on the free tier. This decision should be made at the Month 3 review based on how often the communications team needs to update content and whether they are comfortable with Git-based workflows.
